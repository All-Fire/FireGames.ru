Data Subject Rights

This page explains how to contact the Save The Lost developer about personal data.

1. Requests you may make

To the extent provided by applicable law, you may request:

  • confirmation of whether the developer processes your personal data and access to those data;
  • correction of inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • deletion where there is no lawful reason to continue processing;
  • restriction of or objection to processing;
  • withdrawal of consent where processing is based on consent;
  • information about data categories, purposes and recipients.

2. How to submit a request

Email [email] with the subject “Personal Data Request — Save The Lost”. Please include:

  • the right you wish to exercise;
  • a short description and, where possible, the game version and device model;
  • an email address where the developer can respond.

Do not send passwords, payment details or identity-document copies unless the developer has separately explained a lawful need and a secure method of transfer. The developer may request reasonable additional information if needed to verify your identity or locate data, but should not collect excessive information.

3. What the developer can delete

Under the current reported configuration, the developer does not maintain a database of player accounts or profiles. Game progress and settings may be stored only on your device. To remove local saves, use Android system settings to clear the app's data or uninstall the game. This may permanently delete your progress.

4. Yandex Ads data

Information processed by Yandex and its advertising partners through the advertising SDK may be under the providers' independent control. For requests to Yandex, consult the Yandex Advertising Services Privacy Policy and Yandex Privacy Policy. You may also reset or limit use of the Android advertising ID in device settings, where that option is available.

5. Response

The developer will review your request and respond within the period required by applicable law. If additional information is needed or a request cannot be fully satisfied, the developer will explain this to the extent permitted by law.

6. Supervisory authority

You may lodge a complaint with the competent data-protection authority in your country of habitual residence or another authority provided by applicable law.
